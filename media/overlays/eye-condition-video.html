AMD

Central dark scotoma from macula damage. Peripheral vision remains largely intact. Affects reading and face recognition.

DR

Scattered dark floaters from retinal hemorrhages. General haze and red tint across the field. Blobs drift slowly — simulating vitreous floaters from leaking blood vessels.

GLAUCOMA

Progressive optic nerve damage causes peripheral field loss. Moderate tunnel with a soft, blurred edge — central vision initially preserved.

RP

Inherited photoreceptor degeneration. Severe pinhole tunnel — much tighter than glaucoma — with a hard, sharp edge. Night blindness typical.
